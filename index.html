About the data and definitions
  • Source: Global Dam Watch (GDW) v1.0 barriers table, 41,145 dams (download the data as CSV).
  • Storage is GDW's CAP_MCM (reported, or estimated where no figure was reported), converted from million m³ to km³. Area is AREA_SKM.
  • Values of -99 in the source mean not reported and are left out of sums, medians and charts. Most dams (about 26,000) have no build year, and most have no reported height, so the year filter drops those dams.
  • Main use is MAIN_USE; dams with none are shown as Not reported. Status is TIMELINE; dams with none are shown as Existing.
  • The map plots each dam at its river location (LONG_RIV, LAT_RIV) on a plain longitude–latitude grid.
  • Country outlines come from the world_countries_updated shapefile (based on the US State Department's LSIB), simplified to about 2 km so the page loads quickly, with shared borders kept gap-free (download). Continents follow the usual seven-continent split: Middle East and Caucasus countries are in Asia, Central America and the Caribbean in North America, and Russia is shown whole in Europe because the file has it as one shape.
  • Filters are saved in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
  • Built with D3 (ISC licence, see lib/d3-LICENSE.txt).
